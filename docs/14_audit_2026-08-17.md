## 🔴 ACTED ON DURING THE AUDIT

**1. Coach impersonation (CRITICAL, confirmed live, now mitigated).**
`handle_new_user()` (0005:33-45) assigns `role='coach'` on the sole basis that
the typed email appears in `staff_emails`. Email confirmation is OFF
(`mailer_autoconfirm=true`), and both coach addresses were unclaimed — so
anyone who typed `hfb5af@virginia.edu` at signup got a coach account with read
access to every athlete's pain notes and questions. UVA computing IDs are not
secret. The signup gate does not even require the attacker to be a rostered
athlete: being on *any* allowlist is the ticket, and the coach list is one.

*Mitigation applied:* both coach emails **removed from `staff_emails`** —
verified that registering `hfb5af@virginia.edu` is now rejected. No coach can
be minted while they are parked.

## Coach grid at real scale (never seen above ~5 rows)

- Only ~11 of 30 athletes fit on a 13" laptop; **column headers scroll away** and nothing sticks vertically (`coach/page.tsx:127`).
- **Sticky athlete column is transparent** (`bg-inherit` on a `<tr>` with no base background) — names print on top of mileage the moment the grid scrolls sideways (`coach/page.tsx:185`).
- **Alert strip is unbounded** and not actually pinned: at 15+ entries it pushes the grid below the fold, then scrolls away (`alert-strip.tsx:51`).
- **No handled/answered state** — Sunday's strip still lists every flag dealt with on Tuesday (`queries.ts:303`).
- Strip prints full pain notes but the literal word "question" for questions (`alert-strip.tsx:62`).

## Colour semantics & accessibility

- **Orange no longer means pain.** The "behind goal" bar paints the whole roster orange every Monday, competing with the flag colour (`coach/page.tsx:179`) — and it is *wrong*, comparing week-to-date miles against a full-week goal.
- **The pain signal is the lowest-contrast text in the product** — 2.8:1 orange-on-orange-soft.
- `--color-muted` is 2.47:1 on the page background and carries the entire label layer.
- Red/green reminder vs done semantics are indistinguishable for ~8% of a 30-man team.
- Pain toggle is the smallest control on the form and its label isn't tappable; RPE chips ~27px wide.

## Correctness

- **Midnight ET rollover** writes late-night logs to the wrong day — and on Sunday night, the wrong week (`log/page.tsx:42`).
- Coach week comments are **last-write-wins on full component state** — a stale tab or the second coach silently erases a comment (`coach/actions.ts:102`).
- Importer preview says athletes missing from the Goals tab "keep whatever goal they already had" — on a new week they get **no goal at all** (`upload/actions.ts:97`).
- Goal % capped at 100, hiding over-mileage from both sides (`log/page.tsx:70`).
- `readDate` rolls invalid typed dates over instead of rejecting them, unlike `fromISO` (`importer.ts:74`).

## Remaining security (all lower severity, all confirmed)

- Deleting a row from `staff_emails` **does not revoke** an existing coach — `is_coach()` reads `profiles.role`. The documented go-live step leaves the test coach fully privileged.
- **Any coach can rewrite any profile's role** — mint coaches, demote the head coach, soft-delete him (`0003:21`).
- An athlete can soft-delete their own `athlete_weeks` row; the coach's week comment then saves "successfully" into a row nobody can see (`0003:59`).
- `logs_own` is still `FOR ALL` — athletes can **hard-DELETE a pain flag the coach already read**, and forge entry timestamps (`0001:180`).
- `SECURITY DEFINER` functions set `search_path` without `pg_temp`.
- Service-role key sits in `.env.local` though no code path uses it — free blast radius.
- **No security headers** on any response (no HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy) — measured against production, not in the audit agents' scope.

## Polish

- No `loading.tsx`, no `not-found.tsx` anywhere.
- Nothing typed is persisted until Save — a tapped day chip or a backgrounded phone discards the entry.
- The Sunday nudge shames brand-new athletes with an impossible count and a deadline the app doesn't enforce.
- "Coach sees this today" only appears *after* opting in — the killer feature is invisible on day one.
- 13 font sizes, two spellings of the same value, three near-identical weights; the athlete's two screens have different headers.
- No `color-scheme` declaration — Android Auto Dark Theme will algorithmically invert the app.
- No warning when an uploaded week is in the past (the template's fixed default date makes that the likeliest coach error).
